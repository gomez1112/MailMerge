Mergeform 1.2 brings a fresh new look, a faster way to find your finished PDFs, and dozens of fixes that make every merge more accurate.

New
• A redesigned Mac app with a native sidebar, full Dark Mode support, and smooth animations that follow your Reduce Motion setting
• After a merge, see which folder your PDFs were saved in and open it in Finder with the new files selected
• Cancel a merge at any time, and keep its progress while you move between steps
• Drag and drop templates, spreadsheets, and output folders onto each step
• Format fields as dates, numbers, or currency, with a live example
• PDFs use your template’s paper size, margins, and header logo as they appear in Word
• Mergeform asks before deleting a job (you can turn this off in Settings)

Improved
• Much better compatibility with real-world Excel workbooks: dates, percentages, ZIP codes with leading zeros, formulas, rich text, and TRUE/FALSE now come through exactly as Excel shows them
• Smarter field matching that won’t mix up similar names like “Date” and “State”
• Jobs keep working when you move or rename their template, spreadsheet, or output folder
• Records that would get the same file name are numbered instead of overwriting each other
• The relative dates, auto-match, and default file name settings now take effect
• Lifetime Pro purchases are recognized everywhere in the app

Fixed
• Adding, renaming, and deleting categories in Manage Categories
• Job menu commands acting on the wrong job, and Command-Delete deleting a job while you type
• Changing a job’s template now updates its fields
• Cancelling a subscription purchase no longer closes the upgrade screen
• Many smaller fixes throughout the app
