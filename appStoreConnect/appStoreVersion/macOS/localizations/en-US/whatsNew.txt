Mergeform 1.2.1 makes purchases, spreadsheet formatting, and finished documents more reliable.

New
• Suggest features and vote on ideas from Feature Requests in the sidebar
• Discover other independent Mac apps in the free plan; Pro remains ad-free

Improved
• Native StoreKit purchases and restoration, with immediate Pro access and clearer errors
• Jobs started from the template library are named after their template and have their fields scanned automatically
• Manual field mappings are restored when you switch between sheets

Fixed
• ZIP+4, phone and Social Security formats retain their punctuation
• More accurate conditional, accounting, fraction and scientific number formats
• Date transformations preserve the original Excel date, including its century and day/month order
• Number and currency transformations round consistently with Excel
• Column pickers refresh as soon as spreadsheet headers load
• Spreadsheet reloads handle renamed sheets, and failed previews can be retried
• Email Output keeps each merge’s attachments separate, even for jobs with the same name
• Existing PDFs are preserved; new files receive a numbered name
• Deleting a running job safely stops its merge
• The File menu can open a new window after the last window is closed
